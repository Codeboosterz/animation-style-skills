# A Door Opens — fully authored workflow test

User asked to compare efficiency, expense and repeatability using Hyperframes,
GSAP, JavaScript, Three.js and Suno/Gemini TTS. SAME approved 30-second story,
1920×1080, 30fps. Six approved compositions, no new story. Zero generated video.
Suno instrumental score and one synchronized knock; no voiceover.
Existing 70/30 and full-Wan previews remain untouched.

## Locked design

Huge ivory heads, oversized eyes, bold clean black lines, compact hoodie bodies.
Older charcoal hood-up brother left; younger blue hoodie / brown hair right.
Keep bodies, props, contacts and rooms stationary except allowed actions.
Banned: line boiling, random motion, extra props, orbit, invented dialogue,
caption cards, logos, unplanned gestures and unrelated room reveals.

## Build and sound

GSAP state and Three.js textures/shaders drive all six shots. Bounded hoodie
breathing and registered eyelid/mouth cels: limited 2D animation, not a full
jointed character rig. Door is rigid plane on hinge; hand is a transparent layer.
Local pinned assets, explicit timelines, deterministic from time.

Suno V6 custom instrumental: felt piano/warm strings, tension to hope. Take 2
is 29.84 seconds; padded to 30 and normalized to -13 LUFS before timeline gain.
Music ducks at knock and rises into companionship, closing fade 1.2s. One reused
licensed knock. No TTS required. Requested score structure is not presumed exact;
measure timing/levels and disclose audio not personally auditioned.

Same cuts and runtime. New Kie cost 12 credits for Suno; 0 Wan and 0 TTS.
Two built-in image edits, historical art/rig setup, and subscriptions are separate.
