# A5 style bible

Example: How the Dutch Colonized Suriname, 60s, six segments. The final legacy export is 864×486, 24fps; do not carry that low source tier into a new brief by default.

Look: substantial clean black outlines; white round head with visible continuous jaw/neck connection; adult body approximately four heads tall; compact black mitten hands with one integrated thumb lobe; no detached fingers, white gloves, floating heads or glossy 3D. Softly shaded period clothes over simple stick-cartoon anatomy. Rich but restrained river, plantation, ship, fort, table and forest environments.

Editing: establish location and people → one meaningful physical action → reaction or explanatory insert. The example uses roughly ten-second story sections, with internal cuts where needed. Maps, dates and text are editorial overlays when useful; do not ask the video generator to invent readable historical documents.

History direction: show the region as inhabited before colonists arrive. Keep Indigenous residents, enslaved Africans and Maroon communities distinct. A representative official or worker is not a verified portrait of a named person. Reuse a costume silhouette to establish continuity, but do not present that visual continuity as a documented biography.

Lessons from the ledger tests: first/last-frame references and stricter prose improved staging but did not guarantee a one-way rigid closure. Mini bent the cover and added motion. Omni closed it more convincingly but slid a hand afterward. Both failed strict action compliance. A mechanically exact book belongs on an authored hinge with attached hand anchors when precision matters.
