# A5 Historical Cartoon

Use for period history and documentary explainers. Read [production.md](references/production.md), [style-bible.md](references/style-bible.md) and [shot-direction.md](references/shot-direction.md). The Suriname example is a visual benchmark, not a reusable history script for unrelated subjects.

Research the specific story before writing its script. Distinguish documented events from representative staging. Lock a palette, period wardrobe, cast cards and shot geography in a reviewed storyboard. Use the full A5 anatomy contract in each image/video prompt: white filled round head, connected short white neck entering clothing, roughly four-head adult proportions, head narrower than clothed shoulders, black oval eyes, thin black exposed limbs and compact attached black mitten hands. Sets are full-color painterly environments; clothes have restrained shading. Do not substitute B1 paper backgrounds or big-eyed hoodie characters.

Use approved reference images for generated atmospheric/acting shots, Wan 3.0 when generation is appropriate, and authored GSAP/Three.js mechanics for exact props. Hyperframes owns the master edit and export. Gemini Omni is an optional specialized route when requested or suitable, not an automatic map generator or a guarantee of correct physics.

The bundled historical sample was made before the current workflow decision, with Seedance Mini and a legacy assembly process. Preserve it as history only. New Seedance jobs are prohibited; new A5 films use the current stack. The Wan-based A5 route has not been validated by that old sample—preview the required actions before scaling production.

Fola suits an explicitly narrated history brief. Keep one continuous approved recording and measured pacing; add licensed/suitable ambience and restrained music as requested. Historical suffering is represented with human agency and dignity, not slapstick. Review dates, geography, clothing, group distinctions and any editorial labels separately from animation QA.

The board assets cover the six Suriname beats. They are visual/camera examples, not a full source-media template. Create new topic-specific images and a Hyperframes project when applying this skill.
