# Capsule cinema style bible

Approved reference: Choose Again, 30 seconds, 1080p/30fps, rig refinement v2. User approved the overall film, praised the push-ups and seated ending, and then approved the corrected drawer rest: “Okay, this looks fantastic. Save it.”

Geometry/palette: a cream head about 62×74 local units, tiny dot eyes, simple dark brows/mouth, rounded capsule torso/limbs and mitten hands. Near-black green set #090e0e, cream #e5e1ca, muted contour #74786e, props #20242e. The example uses magenta #c0477b for the habit shadow and warm dawn color only at its ending. Characters are roughly three-to-four heads high; keep proportions consistent between standing, supported and seated poses.

Editing: held wide stages punctuated by one purposeful action. The 30s example condenses bed/phone → morning mirror → drawer boundary → push-ups → calendar setback/recovery → rooftop dawn. Locked camera per shot; captions above action; short, legible phrases rather than continuous transcription. Causal movement and silhouette carry the story.

Motion language: concise anticipation, a directed movement and a settled hold. Fixed hand/foot contacts, no elbow flips, no detached wrists or automatic prop movement. Native code controls exact mechanics; the reference film's pose holds informed timing but were not pasted into the output.

Workflow: native vector geometry and pose functions → paused GSAP state → Three.js CanvasTexture plane → Hyperframes. Suno instrumental + timed Foley. No image-generation fee was incurred for this vector test. Two new Suno requests failed with zero charges, so the approved existing score was reused. This history is not a guarantee of future provider availability or zero total production cost.

Weakest remaining acting beat in the sample: the restrained mirror hold. A new cheek/eye-rub pose may improve a future shot when the brief calls for it; do not silently add it to the approved reference. The style remains limited 2D animation, not a claim of feature-film skeletal or facial simulation.
