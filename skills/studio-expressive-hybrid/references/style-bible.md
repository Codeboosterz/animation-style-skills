# Expressive cartoon style bible

Example: A Door Opens. Older brother in charcoal hoodie, hood up, screen-left; younger in blue hoodie, hood down, screen-right. These identities belong to the example, not all future stories.

Drawing: oversized round ivory heads; huge white eyes with black pupils; bold eyebrows and simple mouth shapes; compact hoodie bodies and short limbs. Navy nighttime interiors resolve to a warm amber lamp. Strong clean contours with restrained shaded fills. No hair/wardrobe changes between shots.

Editing: hallway noticing light → rear over-left-shoulder phone hold → recognition close-up → one knock and door movement → seated connection → wider warm ending. Locked cameras, motivated hard cuts. Preserve the room axis and physical over-shoulder viewpoint. The ending expresses companionship without pretending the underlying struggle is instantly cured.

Authored mechanics: isolated eyelid/cel layers; breathing bounded to 1.6–3 source pixels in torso regions, not whole-image bobbing. Door rotates around its actual right hinge with a registered hand layer. Background and phone remain still. These are demonstrated ranges for this art scale, not mandatory amplitudes for all films.

Generated acting must preserve first-frame references and contacts. The all-Wan edit selected early portions of takes to avoid later invented turns and gestures. That editorial success is not evidence that prompts alone reliably constrain the model.
